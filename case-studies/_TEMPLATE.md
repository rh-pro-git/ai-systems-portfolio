# [Title — the achievement, not the codename]

*[One-sentence hook: what was built and why it matters.]*

**Timeline:** [rough period] · **Stack:** [languages, infra] · **Status:** [running / retired / superseded by X]

## Context

What existed before, and the constraint set (privacy, cost, hardware,
single-operator, etc.) that shaped everything.

## Problem

The specific failure mode or gap. Concrete beats abstract — an incident, a
measurement, a limitation hit in practice.

## Design

The shape of the solution and the boundaries between its parts. A small
diagram is often worth including.

### Alternatives considered

What was rejected and why. This is where engineering judgment shows.

## What made it hard

The two or three genuinely difficult parts, and how they were resolved.

## Selected code

Short excerpts (imported via `tools/import-excerpt.sh`) that illustrate a
decision — not bulk source.

## Outcome

Measured results: what runs, for how long, what it caught or enabled, what it
costs. Include honest limitations.

## Retrospective

What would be done differently now.
